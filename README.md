I am a PhD student in Physics at the Institute of Theoretical Physics-UNESP, transitioning from high-energy particle physics (CMS-CERN) to the study of complex systems and spatial epidemiology.

* **Tools of the Trade:** R-INLA, DLNM, and Geospatial data analysis.
* **Network Science:** Developing weighted spatial graphs to model human mobility and epidemic pathways.
